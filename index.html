SMV Viewer Prototype

A minimal player for the compressed dynamic 3D-Gaussian scenes of SmoothMotionVectors (SIGGRAPH ’26). The motion streams are decoded by ffmpeg.wasm and the splats rasterized with WebGL2, all in your browser.

Pick a run and a scene, then press Load.

Scenes: Neural 3D Video (Li et al., CVPR 2022, neural-3d-video.github.io) and MeetRoom (Li et al., Streaming Radiance Fields for 3D Video Synthesis, NeurIPS 2022, github.com/AlgoHunt/StreamRF; camera poses from Swift4D, Wu et al. 2025), reconstructed and compressed for research demonstration.

Built with ffmpeg.wasm, fflate and a WebGL2 rasterizer after antimatter15’s splat. © 2026 Mingyang Song · CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, except the third-party material listed in LICENSE.
